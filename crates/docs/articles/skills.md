The **Skills** page lists every skill your agents find on this device. Open it from **Skills** in the app rail.

## Find a skill

**All skills** shows everything. The agents under **Harnesses** on the left narrow the list to one. A skill several agents can use, or one linked from several folders, is listed once.

## Open a skill

Click a skill to read its `SKILL.md` in a dialog.

- **Try now** closes the dialog and starts a message with the skill in the composer, on an agent that has it.
- The switch turns the skill on or off for every place it is linked.
- **...** has **Open**, **Reveal in Finder** and **Copy Markdown**.
- **Uninstall** asks first, then **Move to Trash** removes it.

## In the composer

A skill you pick from the `$` menu, or start with **Try now**, shows as a pill in the composer. When you send, the agent gets its `$name`. See [Commands and mentions](zeron://docs/commands-and-mentions).
