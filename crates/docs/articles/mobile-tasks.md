The **Tasks** tab on the iPhone holds your [tasks](zeron://docs/tasks) from every computer: a to-do list you can hand to an agent. Its badge counts the tasks that need you.

![The Tasks tab](ios-tasks.jpg)

## The Tasks list

**List** shows your tasks under **Needs you**, **Working**, **To do** and **Done**, each with its project and due date. Agent trees that are running sit under **Working**, and the ones that finished today under **Done**; tap one to see its outline of agents.

**Kanban** shows the same tasks as columns. Touch and hold a card to move it to another column, which marks it done or not done.

With no tasks yet, the tab says "No tasks yet. Tap + to add one."

## Add a task

Tap **+** for **New task**: a title, notes, where it **Runs on**, and images. **Create** adds it and opens its sheet.

## Open a task

Tap a task that is on a thread to open that thread. Tap any other task to open its sheet. To open the sheet of a task that is on a thread, touch and hold it and choose **Task details**.

![A task's sheet](ios-task-sheet.jpg)

A task's sheet has:

- **Status**, **Due**, **Project** and **Runs on**.
- **Add images**, and **Add a context chat** to give the agent threads to read.
- **Start in a new chat** starts an agent on the task on its computer.
- **Hand to a thread** gives the task to a thread you already have: pick the thread, then **Send the task** to send it as a message, or **Link only**. **Unassign** takes it back; **Open thread** opens the thread it is on.
- **Mark as done** (or **Mark as not done**) and **Delete**, which asks first, are at the bottom.
