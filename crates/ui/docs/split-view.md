Split view shows two sessions side by side, each with its own live transcript and composer. Use it to watch one agent while you talk to another.

## Open a split

Open a session, then drag another session onto the conversation. You can drag a row from the sidebar, a thread row from the Cmd+K palette, or a tab from the titlebar. Two drop zones appear, **Split left** and **Split right**. Drop on one to pair the sessions on that side.

![Two sessions side by side](split-view.jpg)

- Dropping onto the composer instead adds a mention of that thread to your message.
- Drafts, terminal tabs, and the session you already have open cannot be split; their drop zones dim.
- Dropping on a side that already holds a session replaces it. Dropping one half onto the other side swaps them.
- You can have one split at a time. A new pair replaces the old one.

Splitting works by dragging only; there is no menu row or shortcut for it.

## Work in both sides

Type in either composer directly. The side you selected last is the primary one: the right pane and keyboard focus follow it. To make the other side primary, click the split icon in its header, or click its half of the pair's tab.

In the titlebar the pair shows as one double tab. In the sidebar the two rows join into one card labeled **Split**, unless one of them is pinned. Selecting a different session shows it full width, and the pair waits on its tab until you come back.

The **Live state** column is hidden while a split is open, because it would belong to neither side.

## Resize

Drag the seam between the two sides. Each side keeps at least 320 px, and the seam snaps to the middle when you get close. Double-click the seam to return to an even split. Zeron remembers the ratio.

## Close a side

- Click the close button in a side's header to end the split. Closing the secondary side keeps the primary full width; closing the primary side gives the whole column to the other session.
- Click the close button on the sidebar **Split** card to end the split.
- Close one half of the double tab, or press Cmd+W, to close that session's tab. The other session stays open full width.

Ending a split never stops either session. Archiving or deleting one of them also ends the split.
