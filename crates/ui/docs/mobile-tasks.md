The **Tasks** tab on the iPhone holds your [tasks](zeron://docs/tasks) and [missions](zeron://docs/mission-control) from every computer. Its badge counts the questions and approvals waiting on you there.

![The Tasks tab, and a mission's plan review](ios-tasks.jpg)

## The Tasks list

From the top:

- The **Supervisor** card: what the Supervisor watches, on which computers, and when it last woke. Tap it for the Supervisor page (see [The Supervisor on iPhone](zeron://docs/mobile-inbox)).
- **Missions**: each mission with its computer, its state (such as **Needs you · plan review**) and its step count. The agents working on it show as brand marks.
- **Needs you**, **Working**, **To do** and **Done**: your tasks, each with its project and due date.

With no tasks yet, the tab says "No tasks yet. Tap + to add one."

## Add a task

Tap **+** for **New task**: a title, where it **Runs on**, images, and **Plan first** to make it a mission that a lead plans before it runs. With **Plan first** on, **Let me review the plan before it runs** holds the plan for you. **Create** adds it; a mission opens on its mission page.

You can also start a mission from the composer with **Start mission** (see [The composer on iPhone](zeron://docs/mobile-composer)).

## A task's sheet

Tap a task to open its sheet.

![A task's sheet, and a step's actions on a mission page](ios-task-sheet.jpg)

- **Status**, **Due**, **Project** and **Runs on**.
- **Add images**, and **Add a context chat** to give the agent threads to read.
- **Start in a new chat** starts an agent on the task on its computer.
- **Hand to a thread** gives the task to a thread you already have: pick the thread, then **Send the task** to send it as a message, or **Link only**. **Unassign** takes it back; **Open thread** opens the thread it is on.
- **Open mission page**, for a task that is a mission.
- **Mark as done** (or **Mark as not done**) and **Delete**, which asks first, are at the bottom.

## A mission's page

Tap a mission to open its **Mission page**. On top, the mission's state and how many steps are done.

**Plan review**: when the lead has a plan, it reads "Plan ready · N steps · the lead waits for you". Below it:

- The cost estimate, such as "Relative cost 3.2x vs 6.0x if all ran on the lead's" model, the mix of models the steps run on, and a line when a step fell back to another model.
- The plan itself, rendered as Markdown, then **Approve and run**.

**Steps**: each step with its status, any open question, its model and the agent's brand mark. A running mission names the model each step ran on. Tap a step for **Open thread**, **Brief**, **Retry**, **Skip** or **Unskip**.

**Edit** in the title bar lets you rename, add, delete and reorder planned steps. Send the changes to the lead, approve them with **Approve and run with** the changes, or **Discard changes**.

**Open lead thread** opens the lead's chat.

The **…** menu has **Watched by the Supervisor**, **Authority on** that computer (see [The Supervisor on iPhone](zeron://docs/mobile-inbox)), and **Lead brief**: the lead's brief, with the messages to and from the lead.

When the Supervisor approved the plan for you, the mission shows "Approved by the Supervisor · starts in" a countdown, with **Take back**, before the lead hears of it. See [The Supervisor](zeron://docs/supervisor).

## Threads in a mission

A thread that belongs to a mission shows a strip at the top with the mission, its step and its progress. Tap it to open the mission's page. In the session's **…** menu, **Open mission page** does the same.
